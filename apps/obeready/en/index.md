# Quick guide – oBeReady

oBeReady is used to plan and run training on operational barrier elements (OBE):
choose a scenario (DFU), choose OBEs, set roles, send out self-preparation, run
the table-top and the exercise, and evaluate the training. The app also has a
training matrix and a yearly check-out per role.

You must be signed in with your Equinor account. Menus and texts are shown in
Norwegian or English depending on your language setting. The actions you can
perform depend on your role.

## Open the app

Prod: Coming

## The first time you open the app

1. Read the privacy notice and choose **Accept**. If you choose **Decline**, you
   cannot use the app. You can withdraw consent under **Privacy settings**.
2. Choose the plant you work at and choose **Save plant**. The choice is saved
   for your next visit.

You change plant or open the privacy settings from the cog at the top right of
the start page (**Change plant** / **Privacy settings**).

## The start page

The start page has three choices:

- **OBE training:** an overview of trainings, and planning of new ones.
- **Training matrix:** which OBEs belong to a DFU and a role.
- **Yearly check-out per role:** confirm the OBEs that belong to your roles.

## 1. Plan a new training

1. Choose **OBE training**, then **Plan new training**.
2. Choose the DFU relevant for the trip. Use **View OBEs** to see which OBEs the
   scenario trains at your plant.
3. Choose the scenario. The training is created and you land on the training
   page.

## 2. Run the training

The training page follows a process bar with seven steps. Use **Previous** and
**Next** at the bottom to move between the steps.

1. **Scenario:** Describe the scenario, the training moment and the learning
   goals. Add a description of the table-top and the exercise, with images and
   links. Set the **Table-top date**: with a date the training is *Planned*,
   without one it is a *Draft*. Changes are saved automatically.
2. **Choose OBEs:** Drag the OBEs you want to focus on from the list above to
   the list of chosen OBEs, or use the arrows.
3. **Set roles:** Choose the people responsible for each role.
   - Switch between **POB only** and **All people** when you search.
   - People who are not linked to an Equinor user have a **Link user** button.
     Search for and confirm the right person.
   - The roles are locked once the self-preparation has been sent out.
4. **Self-preparation:** Check the text and choose **Send self-preparation**.
   Each recipient gets a card in Teams and confirms with **Confirm**. Under
   recipients you see the status: *Sent*, *Confirmed* or *Not delivered*. You
   cannot send to people without an e-mail address or a linked user.
5. **Table-top:** Run the table-top together with the participants. Use
   **Show full screen** for the presentation with cards for scenario, training
   moment and learning goals, and images and links in a panel on the right.
6. **Exercise:** Run the exercise the same way.
7. **Evaluation:** Choose **Send evaluations**, choose a role and people, and
   confirm. Each person gets a card in Teams. Use **Refresh** to fetch new
   answers, search by name or role, and filter on *Answered* or *Waiting*.

## 3. Complete, reopen or delete a training

Use the actions on the training page:

- **Mark as completed:** The training is locked for changes, but you can still
  send out evaluations.
- **Reopen training:** Opens a completed training for editing again.
- **Delete:** Removes the training from the overview. Its content is kept.

## 4. Find trainings

Under **OBE training** you see the recent trainings for the chosen plant. Use the
search field (ID, scenario, title or name), the scenario filter and **More
filters** (status, training date, creator and created date). Choose a training to
open it.

## 5. Training matrix

Choose **Training matrix**, choose a DFU and optionally a role, and search the
OBE list to see which OBEs apply.

## 6. Yearly check-out per role

1. Choose **Yearly check-out per role**. You see whether the check-out is valid,
   ready to be carried out, or not done.
2. Choose **Start new check-out** (or **Start yearly check-out**).
3. Step 1: choose your installation and the roles you are confirming OBEs for,
   and choose **Start check-out**.
4. Step 2: go through the OBEs. OBEs you have already confirmed through
   self-preparation for a table-top or exercise are shown as previously
   confirmed.
5. Choose **Confirm check-out**.

Under **History** you see completed yearly check-outs and self-preparations,
grouped by year.

## Help and feedback

At the bottom of the app you find **What's new** (version history), **Give
feedback on the app** and **OBE improvement suggestions**. The footer shows
which version you are using.
